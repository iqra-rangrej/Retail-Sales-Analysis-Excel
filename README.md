# 🛍️ Retail Sales Analysis & Interactive Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes retail transaction data using Microsoft Excel to understand sales performance, product profitability, customer segments, payment preferences, monthly sales trends, and city-wise revenue.

The project follows a complete data analysis workflow, starting with data cleaning and preparation, followed by lookup functions, calculated columns, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and an interactive dashboard.

The main objective is to transform raw retail data into meaningful business insights that help businesses evaluate performance, identify opportunities, and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales revenue, profit, and quantity sold.
- Identify the product categories generating the highest sales and profit.
- Understand monthly sales fluctuations and performance patterns.
- Compare revenue across different customer segments.
- Analyze customer payment method preferences.
- Identify the top five cities by sales revenue.
- Build an interactive dashboard for monitoring key business performance indicators.

## 📊 Project Workflow & Business Analysis

### 1. Data Cleaning and Preparation

[📷 View Clean Data Screenshot](screenshots/01_Clean_Data.png)

![Clean Data](screenshots/01_Clean_Data.png)

**Business Analysis Performed**

The raw retail dataset was prepared for analysis by removing duplicate records, handling missing values, standardizing customer names, correcting date formats, and ensuring that relevant numeric fields were stored in appropriate formats.

The original raw data was retained separately, while the cleaned dataset was used for subsequent analysis.

**How This Helps the Business**

- Reduces the risk of inaccurate reporting caused by duplicate records.
- Improves consistency in customer names, dates, and other fields.
- Makes sales transactions easier to filter, summarize, and analyze.
- Provides a more reliable foundation for business reporting and decision-making.

### 2. Product Enrichment and Additional Calculated Columns

[📷 View Additional Columns Screenshot](screenshots/02_Clean_Data_Additional_Columns.png)

![Additional Calculated Columns](screenshots/02_Clean_Data_Additional_Columns.png)

**Business Analysis Performed**

Product information was added to the cleaned dataset using VLOOKUP and INDEX-MATCH functions. Additional calculated columns were created to calculate Sales, Cost, Profit, Profit Margin, Profit Status, and Sales Category.

These calculations connect individual transactions with their financial performance, making it possible to evaluate revenue and profitability at the transaction level.

**How This Helps the Business**

- Calculates sales revenue after applying discounts.
- Identifies the cost and profit associated with transactions.
- Helps compare profitability across products and categories.
- Uses profit margin to understand the percentage of sales retained as profit.
- Classifies transactions into High, Medium, and Low sales categories for easier analysis.

### 3. PivotTables — Business Performance Summary

[📷 View PivotTables Screenshot](screenshots/03_Pivot_Tables.png)

![PivotTables](screenshots/03_Pivot_Tables.png)

**Business Analysis Performed**

PivotTables were created to summarize the transaction data across six important business dimensions:

- **Sales by Category:** Compares sales revenue from Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.
- **Sales by Customer Segment:** Measures the contribution of Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office customers.
- **Sales by Payment Method:** Compares Cash, Credit Card, Net Banking, and UPI sales.
- **Sales by City:** Evaluates revenue generated across different cities.
- **Profit by Category:** Compares the profit generated by each product category.
- **Monthly Sales:** Summarizes sales revenue by month.

**Key Business Findings**

- Technology contributes the highest sales revenue and profit among the three categories in the displayed results.
- Consumer customers generate the highest revenue among the three customer segments.
- Sales are distributed across all four payment methods, allowing the business to understand payment preferences.
- City-wise sales totals help identify stronger markets and locations that may require further investigation.
- Monthly sales figures show fluctuations in revenue throughout the year.

**How This Helps the Business**

PivotTables make it easier to summarize thousands of transaction records without manually calculating every total. They help managers compare business performance, identify leading categories, understand customer contributions, and investigate geographic and monthly sales patterns.

### 4. Data Visualization — Turning Data into Insights

[📷 View Data Visualization Screenshot](screenshots/04_Data_Visualization.png)

![Data Visualization](screenshots/04_Data_Visualization.png)

**Business Analysis Performed**

Charts were created to communicate the results of the analysis visually.

- **Sales by Category:** Highlights the differences in revenue across product categories.
- **Monthly Sales Trend:** Shows how revenue changes from January to December.
- **Sales by Customer Segment:** Compares revenue contributed by different customer groups.
- **Sales by Payment Method:** Shows the proportion of sales associated with each payment method.

**Key Business Findings**

- Technology is the leading category by sales revenue and profit in the displayed analysis.
- Consumer customers contribute the largest revenue among the three customer segments.

**How This Helps the Business**

Visualizations make complex data easier to understand and communicate. They help managers identify patterns, compare performance, investigate weaker sales periods, and prioritize areas for further analysis.

For example, lower sales in a particular month could prompt a business to investigate product availability, customer demand, promotions, or seasonal factors before deciding on corrective action.

### 5. Interactive Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

[📷 View Dashboard Screenshot](screenshots/05_Dashboard.png)

![Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard](screenshots/05_Dashboard.png)

**Business Analysis Performed**

The dashboard combines key performance indicators, charts, and interactive filters into one reporting interface.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

- **Total Sales:** Measures the overall sales revenue recorded in the dataset.
- **Total Profit:** Shows the total profit calculated from sales and costs.
- **Total Orders:** Counts the order records included in the analysis.
- **Total Quantity Sold:** Measures the total quantity sold across the transactions.

**Dashboard Visualizations**

- Monthly Sales Performance
- Sales Distribution by Product Category
- Payment Method Distribution
- Revenue by Customer Segment
- Profitability by Product Category
- Top 5 Cities by Sales Revenue

The dashboard also includes slicers for payment method, city, category, and customer segment, along with an Order Date timeline for exploring the available data.

**Key Business Findings**

- KPI cards provide a quick overview of the business's overall performance.
- Category charts highlight the product groups contributing most to sales and profit.
- Customer segment analysis identifies the groups generating the largest revenue.
- Monthly sales trends make changes in performance easier to investigate.
- The Top 5 Cities chart highlights the leading cities by sales revenue in the displayed results.

**How This Helps the Business**

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of important business metrics, reducing the need to review several separate tables and charts.

Managers can use it to monitor performance, compare product categories, examine customer segments, and explore geographic or time-based sales patterns. Interactive filters make it easier to focus on specific areas of the business and support more informed planning.
